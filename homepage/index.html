Community Pillars

  • No judgment: Caregiving brings exhaustion, grief, and anger. All feelings are valid here.
  • Protect privacy: Refrain from sharing full legal names or medical record details.
  • Validation over correction: What works for one stage may differ for another. Listen with empathy.
