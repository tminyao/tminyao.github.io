💡 Ways to Reduce Daily Burden

1. De-escalate Sundowning

  • Light Therapy: Turn on bright indoor lights before dusk so shadows do not trigger disorientation.
  • Consistent Sounds: Play low-tempo ambient music or familiar radio shows starting at 4:30 PM.
  • Avoid Debates: Do not argue with memory lapses; validate their underlying emotion instead.

2. Protect Against Burnout

  • The 5-Minute Box Breathing: Inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s, hold 4s. Resets high heart rates.
  • Set Guilt-Free Boundaries: Accept that good care does not mean flawless care.
  • Micro-Breaks: Step into another room for 3 minutes to recover your emotional composure.

3. Arrange Respite Support

  • Adult Day Centers: Provides structured sensory activities while you get 6 hours of personal rest.
  • In-Home Companions: Hire an aide for two afternoons a week to catch up on sleep or errands.
  • Local Circles: Join peer caregiver meetings to swap practical, localized coping tips.
